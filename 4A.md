## 2. Hard Negative Mining breakdown

To train a robust neural matcher on repeating lattices, we generated 4 types of negatives evenly distributed:
- **`random`**: Completely unrelated locations (easy negatives).
- **`nearby`**: High-drift spatial offsets (10 to 50px away in canvas coordinates).
- **`repeated`**: Shifts aligned exactly to 1 or 2 repeating grid pitches (very hard negatives).
- **`wrong_geom`**: Correct center but incorrect rotation ($10^\circ \text{ to } 45^\circ$) or scale ($\pm 15\%$) (hard geometry negatives).

## 3. Data Leakage and Separation Check
- **✓ Benchmark Set Isolated**: The frozen benchmark (40 samples generated with seed `42`) remains completely untouched and isolated.
- **✓ Split Independence**: Each split (`train`, `val`, `dev`) uses a distinct master seed (`1000`, `2000`, `3000`) and procedural sub-seeds to generate separate, non-overlapping base wafer canvases, ensuring no leakage between subsets.
